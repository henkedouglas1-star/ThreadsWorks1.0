## 4. Source Integrity & Curriculum Decisions
* **Original source:** The Cash Machine PDF is 1,749 pages. The source advertises “99 classes,” while the accessible curriculum numbering extends through Class 220.
* **Class 125:** The accessible source numbering is missing Class 125. A new class was authored: *Choosing The Right Acquisition Strategy*.
* **Classes 171–197:** These classes are missing from the accessible source material. A new 27-class bridge was authored as *Sales Optimization & Operating System* to connect Sales Measurement & Management with Advanced Sales & Revenue Mastery.
* **Important:** Newly authored material must not be represented as recovered source text. It was designed to preserve the terminology, progression, teaching philosophy, and structure of the Cash Machine.
* **Implementation rule:** The original 1,749-page PDF remains attached as the source document for the eventual knowledge base.
